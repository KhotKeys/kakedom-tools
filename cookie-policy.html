Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 4, 2026

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Käkedom Tools uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and platform. We believe in being transparent about how we collect and use data, and this policy provides detailed information about the cookies we use and why.

By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time using the Cookie Settings option in our footer.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and gather information about website usage.

Cookies typically contain:

  • The name of the website that created the cookie
  • A unique identifier
  • An expiration date
  • Information relevant to the cookie's purpose

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for our website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. Without these cookies, certain services you request cannot be provided.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
session_id Maintains your login session and user authentication Session (deleted when browser closes)
csrf_token Security measure to prevent cross-site request forgery attacks Session
cookie_consent Remembers your cookie preferences 1 year

Note: You cannot refuse these cookies, as they are necessary for the website to function. However, you can configure your browser to block or delete them, though this may affect your ability to use our services.

3.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect information about how visitors use our website, such as which pages are visited most often and whether users encounter error messages. This helps us improve the performance and user experience of our platform.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics - Distinguishes unique users 2 years
_gid Google Analytics - Distinguishes users 24 hours
_gat Google Analytics - Throttles request rate 1 minute

The information collected by these cookies is aggregated and anonymous. It tells us things like:

  • How many people visit our website
  • Which pages are most popular
  • How long users spend on different pages
  • Where visitors are located (country/region level only)

3.3 Functionality Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make (such as your language preference or region) and provide enhanced, personalized features.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
user_preferences Stores your display and notification preferences 6 months
language Remembers your language selection 1 year
location Stores your location for localized farming advice 3 months

3.4 Targeting and Advertising Cookies (Currently Not Used)

Important Notice: Käkedom Tools currently does NOT use targeting or advertising cookies. We do not track you across websites for advertising purposes, and we do not sell your data to advertisers.

If we decide to implement such cookies in the future, we will update this policy and request your explicit consent.

Some cookies on our website are set by third-party services we use:

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as page views, time spent on pages, and navigation paths. This data is anonymous and helps us improve our platform.

4.2 Social Media Cookies

We have social media sharing buttons on our website (Facebook, LinkedIn). These platforms may set cookies when you interact with these buttons. We do not control these cookies.

  • Facebook: Facebook Cookie Policy

5. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To keep you logged in as you navigate our platform
  • Security: To protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  • Performance: To understand how our website performs and identify areas for improvement
  • User Experience: To remember your preferences and provide personalized content
  • Analytics: To gather insights about user behavior and website traffic
  • Troubleshooting: To diagnose and fix technical issues

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

6.1 Cookie Settings on Our Website

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" button in our website footer. This allows you to:

  • View which cookies are active
  • Enable or disable non-essential cookies
  • Update your preferences at any time

Note: Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

6.2 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • View and delete cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies from specific websites
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

6.3 Mobile Devices

On mobile devices, you can manage cookies through your browser app settings or device settings:

  • iOS (Safari): Settings → Safari → Block Cookies
  • Android (Chrome): Chrome app → Settings → Site settings → Cookies

7. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Currently, there is no industry standard for how to respond to DNT signals. Käkedom Tools respects your privacy choices, but our website does not specifically respond to DNT signals at this time.

8. Data Retention

Different cookies have different lifespans:

  • Session cookies: Automatically deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies: Remain on your device for a set period (ranging from minutes to years) or until you manually delete them

We regularly review and delete cookies that are no longer necessary for our operations.

9. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our practices. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Notify you via email (if you have an account with us)
  • Display a prominent notice on our website

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.

10. Your Rights

Under data protection laws, you have rights regarding cookies and tracking technologies:

  • Right to Information: You have the right to know what cookies are used and why
  • Right to Refuse: You can refuse non-essential cookies (though some features may not work)
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can change your cookie preferences at any time
  • Right to Access: You can request information about the data collected through cookies
